## 1. Introduction

My Little CRM ("the App") is a mobile customer relationship management application developed and published by RePass Cloud Pty Ltd ("RePass Cloud", "we", "us" or "our"), an Australian company.

We respect your privacy and believe that your business information should remain under your control.

My Little CRM is designed to operate locally on your device without requiring an account, internet connection or cloud-based storage.

**We do not collect, transmit, store or share your CRM data on our servers.**

This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information, where your data is stored, and the choices available to you.

This policy supplements the [RePass Cloud Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy/). Where the two policies differ regarding My Little CRM, this application-specific policy takes precedence.

## 2. Information You Enter Into the App

My Little CRM allows you to create, manage and maintain customer relationship information.

Depending on how you use the App, this information may include:

- Customer and business names.
- Contact names, telephone numbers and email addresses.
- Business addresses and other contact details.
- Notes, interactions and customer relationship records.
- Other information you voluntarily enter or import into the App.

This information may contain personal information about you, your customers or other individuals.

**All information entered into My Little CRM is stored locally on your device.**

RePass Cloud does not receive, access, monitor or maintain copies of this information.

You remain responsible for ensuring that any personal information you enter into the App is collected and used in accordance with applicable privacy laws and your own business obligations.

## 3. Information We Collect

My Little CRM does not independently collect personal information from you or your device.

Specifically, the App does not:

- Require registration or a user account.
- Request your name, email address or telephone number for registration.
- Collect your device's location.
- Access your contacts automatically.
- Collect advertising identifiers.
- Monitor your activity or usage patterns.
- Use analytics or behavioural tracking services.
- Use third-party advertising services.
- Transmit your customer records to RePass Cloud.
- Upload your information to external databases or cloud services.
- Include integrated crash-reporting or telemetry services that transmit information to us.

The App does not require access to our servers to perform its core functions.

Information handled separately through app marketplaces, operating-system services or voluntary communications with RePass Cloud is explained below.

## 4. Local Data Storage

My Little CRM uses an on-device SQLite database to store information entered into the App.

Your CRM information remains on your device and is managed through the App's local storage.

RePass Cloud does not operate a remote database or cloud synchronisation service for My Little CRM.

This means:

- Your information is available locally through the App.
- Your customer records are not automatically synchronised with RePass Cloud.
- RePass Cloud employees and administrators cannot view your locally stored records.
- We cannot remotely access, modify, retrieve or restore your CRM database.

Your data may also be subject to your device's operating-system security, backup and recovery mechanisms.

We recommend keeping your device secure and maintaining appropriate backups of important business information.

## 5. File Import and Export

My Little CRM may allow you to select files from your device and export information into supported file formats, including PDF documents.

### File imports

When you choose a file using the App's file selection functionality:

- You control which file is selected.
- The App accesses the selected file for the requested operation.
- Any information imported into the CRM is processed and stored locally.
- The selected file and its contents are not transmitted to RePass Cloud.

The App does not automatically scan your device for unrelated documents or files.

### File exports

When you export information from My Little CRM:

- The exported file is generated locally.
- You control where the file is saved or shared using the options available on your device.
- RePass Cloud does not receive a copy of the exported file.
- Exported files may contain personal or commercially sensitive information.

If you choose to save an exported file to a cloud storage provider, email it, or share it with another application, that provider or application may process the information according to its own privacy policy.

You are responsible for managing and protecting files that you export or share.

## 6. Internet Access and Third-Party Services

My Little CRM is designed to function without an internet connection.

The App does not use internet connectivity to transmit CRM records, user activity or device information to RePass Cloud or third-party analytics providers.

The App does not integrate advertising networks, online tracking systems or remote CRM storage services.

However, your device's operating system, application marketplace, backup provider or another application you choose to interact with may independently process information.

These activities are controlled by the relevant third-party service and are subject to its own terms and privacy policies.

## 7. Data Sharing

**RePass Cloud does not share, sell, rent or disclose information stored within My Little CRM because we do not receive or have access to that information.**

The App does not automatically share your CRM data with advertisers, analytics providers, data brokers or other third parties.

You may voluntarily export or share information through your device's available file-sharing functionality.

Any such disclosure is initiated and controlled by you.

## 8. Data Security

My Little CRM stores its application data within the storage environment provided by your device's operating system.

The App relies on the operating system's application isolation, access controls and device security mechanisms to help protect locally stored information.

We recommend that you:

- Enable a device passcode, password or biometric authentication.
- Keep your operating system updated.
- Avoid installing applications from untrusted sources.
- Protect exported files containing customer or business information.
- Maintain appropriate backups of important records.

Local storage does not guarantee protection against every security threat, particularly if a device is compromised, lost or accessed without authorisation.

RePass Cloud cannot remotely secure, inspect or recover information stored exclusively on your device.

## 9. Data Retention and Deletion

Your CRM information remains on your device until you remove it through the App's available functionality or your device's storage management options.

You may remove locally stored application data by:

- Deleting individual records where supported by the App.
- Clearing the App's storage through your device settings.
- Uninstalling the App, subject to your operating system's data retention and backup behaviour.

RePass Cloud does not retain server-side copies of your CRM records and cannot delete them remotely.

Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage may permanently remove your locally stored information.

**You are responsible for backing up or exporting any information you wish to retain before deleting the App or its data.**

Files previously exported, shared or included in operating-system backups may continue to exist independently of the App and must be managed separately.

## 10. Device Backups and Cloud Services

Your device may offer automatic backup, restoration or cloud storage functionality.

Depending on your device configuration, these services may include application data or files generated by My Little CRM.

Such backups are managed by your operating system or selected backup provider, not by RePass Cloud.

RePass Cloud does not control how third-party backup providers store, retain, transfer or delete that information.

You should review your device's backup settings and the privacy policies of any backup or cloud storage services you use.

## 11. Children's Privacy

My Little CRM is intended as a general-purpose business productivity application and is not specifically designed for children.

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children or transmit such information to RePass Cloud.

Because the App operates locally without user registration or tracking, RePass Cloud does not independently determine the age of individual users.

## 12. Application Marketplace Information

My Little CRM may be distributed through third-party application marketplaces, including Google Play and the Apple App Store.

These marketplaces may independently collect or process information associated with downloading, installing, purchasing, updating or reviewing applications.

Such information is governed by the applicable marketplace provider's privacy policy.

Any information processed by the marketplace does not mean that My Little CRM independently collects or transmits CRM data.

## 13. Contacting Us and Privacy Requests

If you contact RePass Cloud for assistance, feedback or a privacy-related enquiry, we may receive the information you voluntarily provide.

This may include your name, email address, message content and any attachments you choose to submit.

We handle these communications in accordance with our main RePass Cloud Privacy Policy.

Please avoid sending customer records, confidential business information or sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your enquiry.

Because CRM records are stored locally, we cannot directly access, correct, export or delete information held exclusively within your device's application database.

My Little CRM may be available to users outside Australia.

Because CRM information is stored locally and is not transmitted to RePass Cloud, we do not independently transfer your locally stored CRM records internationally.

However, information may be transferred or processed internationally if you choose to export or share it using external services, or if your operating-system backup provider processes data in another country.

Applicable privacy and data protection laws may provide you with additional rights.

Nothing in this policy limits any rights available under applicable law.
