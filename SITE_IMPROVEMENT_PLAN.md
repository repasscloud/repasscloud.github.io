# RePass Cloud Website Improvement Plan — SUPERSEDED

> **Superseded:** 2026-08-17
> **Superseded by:** `docs/REPASSCLOUD-SITE-REBUILD-CLAUDE-PROMPT.md`

This document previously recommended a services-first, engagement-funnel direction for
repasscloud.com — an "Engagements" section with consulting packages and durations, more
persistent contact CTAs, and a services-first homepage.

That direction has been explicitly rejected and reversed. The site is now positioned primarily as
a software company that designs, builds, owns, and operates its own products (see
`docs/REPASSCLOUD-SITE-REBUILD-CLAUDE-PROMPT.md` for the full brief), with a first-class Products
section, a quieter Contact/CTA presence, and enterprise engineering capability kept as supporting
content rather than the primary identity.

Do not act on the recommendations that were previously in this file. See `CLAUDE.md` for the
current, authoritative repository direction.
