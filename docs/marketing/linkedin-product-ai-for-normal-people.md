# LinkedIn Product Page: AI for Normal People

Fields for adding the book as a product on the RePass Cloud company page (Page admin view → Products → Add product). Field list and limits come from LinkedIn's Product Pages getting-started guide.

LinkedIn built Product Pages for B2B software, so its category list is software-focused, and LinkedIn reviews each page before it goes live. If no category fits a book, see the note under "Product category".

---

## Product name

```
AI for Normal People
```

LinkedIn may pre-fill the name. If it pre-fills something else, request a change through the link in the form, or email productpages-feedback@linkedin.com.

---

## Product category

Required. LinkedIn won't approve the page if this is blank.

Pick the closest option to education or e-learning in LinkedIn's list. If nothing fits a book, use the "request a product category adjustment" link and ask for:

```
Books / educational publishing (non-technical guide to artificial intelligence)
```
